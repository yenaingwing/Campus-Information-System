Laboratory(Building A)

Location: Located near northwest border of UCSM's campus
Description:

The computer science lab at UCSM is in the northwest part of the campus. It's where students use modern tech for hands-on experiments, learning about programming, algorithms, and networking. Teachers guide them through practical tasks, helping them build practical skills. It's a space where students work together, solve problems, and apply what they learn to real-world situations, preparing for careers in computer science. There is only one way that can reach laboratory from main building.It is 580 meters long.

Location: Located near northwest border of UCSM's campus

Nestled in the northwest quadrant of the University of Computer Studies, Mandalay (UCSM) campus.At UCSM's physics laboratory, students engage in a variety of experiments using standard equipment.
